## clash-of-clans-data

The Home Village game data in `src/data/buildings.json` and `src/data/traps.json` is derived from
[`clash-of-clans-data` 0.16.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/clash-of-clans-data),
copyright 2026 Christopher Pezza, licensed under the MIT License.

The source dataset was generated on June 15, 2026. Clash Tool keeps stable
database IDs and German display names while retaining the upstream `sourceId`
for future synchronization.

Village recognition uses numerical image descriptors derived from
[`clash-of-clans-data` 0.19.1](https://www.npmjs.com/package/clash-of-clans-data)
and [Supercell's November 2026 update images](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/whats-new-cc-nov26.html).
